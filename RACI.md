# Stakeholders & RACI

## Where the architect sits
The architect is **subordinate to strategy and lateral to peers**. The developer's
board decides *why* (build the park, fund readiness); the architect owns the *how*
(design, specification), validated by engineering, facilities and procurement, and
delivered by project and integrator teams. The architect influences broadly and
decides narrowly — R for the design, C almost everywhere else.

## Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Developer board | Yield, vacancy, differentiation | Decides & funds |
| Development / engineering (landlord) | Buildability, schedule | Validates, can block |
| Commercial / leasing | Sellable readiness | Consumes the proposition |
| Facilities / operations | Long-term operability | Owns run-cost |
| Anchor & other tenants | Continuity, fairness | Accept interface |
| Construction contractor | Executes civil | Consumes specs |
| Integrator (our employer) | Scope & margin | Designs & oversees |
| Carrier / ISP | Feasibility | External, long lead |

## RACI — key deliverables
R responsible · A accountable · C consulted · I informed

| Deliverable | Architect | Head of Pre-Sales / Solutions | Engineering | Projects | Procurement | Facilities |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery, HLD, ADRs | **R** | **A** | C | I | I | C |
| Interface spec | **R** | A | C | C | I | C |
| BoM/BoQ & vendor selection | C | A | C | I | **R** | C |
| Critical power delivery | C | A | C | R | C | **R/A** |
| Meet-me & backbone build | C | A | **R** | R | C | C |
| Interface acceptance | C | A | C | R | I | C |
| TCO/ROI & proposal | **R** | **A** | I | I | C | I |

The architect is **R** for design and proposal, **C** for delivery — exactly the role
boundary. Delivery is owned by Projects and Facilities, funded by the board, gated by
Engineering and Procurement.
