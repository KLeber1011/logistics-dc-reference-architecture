# Statement of Work (conceptual)

> Conceptual SOW — demonstrates how scope, roles and boundaries are framed, in the
> language integrators and clients transact in. Not a contract.

## Objective
Design and specify the shared technology infrastructure for a multi-tenant logistics
condominium, and defend it commercially, so modules lease as technology-ready assets.

## In scope
Discovery; HLD; tenant interface specification; BoM/BoQ & LOE; TCO/ROI; cloud on-ramp
design; site-survey methodology; roadmap & dependencies; risk register; governance;
technical proposal and executive defence; oversight of delivery and acceptance.

## Out of scope
Civil/structural works; tenant internal networks (Wi-Fi, OT, IoT, RFID, CCTV, WMS);
automation equipment selection; fire detection/suppression engineering; physical
security manpower; carrier last-mile civil execution; tenant cloud architecture.

## Deliverables
D1 Discovery · D2 HLD · D3 Interface Spec · D4 BoM/BoQ · D5 TCO/ROI · D6 Cloud Connect ·
D7 Technical Proposal · D8 Site Survey · D9 Roadmap · D10 Risks · ADRs · RACI · lab
evidence.

## Roles (summary; full RACI in RACI.md)
Architect owns design & proposal; Head of Pre-Sales accountable; Engineering validates;
Projects/Facilities deliver; Procurement contracts; Carrier delivers last mile.

## Boundary note
The architect's authority ends at design and specification; delivery is owned by
Projects and Facilities and gated by Engineering, Procurement and Security. Making that
boundary explicit is where multi-tenant programs succeed or fail.
