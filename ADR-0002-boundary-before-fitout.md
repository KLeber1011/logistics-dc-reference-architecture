# ADR-0002 — The interface boundary is agreed and quantified before fit-out

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Spare fibre, rack units, kVA, containment separation and antenna rights are cheap to
provide during civil works and expensive-to-impossible to retrofit into an occupied,
operating module.

## Decision
The landlord↔tenant interface is **quantified and signed before fit-out**: specific
fibre pairs, U, kVA, containment entries and antenna positions per module, versioned
and change-controlled.

## Consequences
**Positive:** no retrofit disputes; capital spent once; tenant can plan its build on
firm commitments.
**Trade-off:** requires discipline and early tenant engagement, sometimes before a
tenant is even signed — met by a standard template that assumes a generic tenant.

## Notes
Turns "we'll sort it out later" — the most expensive phrase in multi-tenant — into a
baseline.
